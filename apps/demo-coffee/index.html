Since 2019

We roast in an old bakery.

Marta and Jakub took over the back room of a closed bakery in Brno with a secondhand 12-kilo roaster and a plan to sell coffee to the neighbours. The bread oven was too heavy to move, so it stayed. Its arch now shelters the roaster, and our green coffee sacks dry out where the loaves used to.

We are five people now. We still roast twice a week, taste every batch before it is bagged, and write the roast date on every bag by hand.
