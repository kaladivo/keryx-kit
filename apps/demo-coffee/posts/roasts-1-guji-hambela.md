Our first Ethiopian of the season landed in Brno last week, and we roasted the first batch on Friday.

**Guji Hambela** comes from smallholders around Hambela Wamena, in the Guji zone, grown at about 2,100 metres. The cherries are washed and dried on raised beds for twelve days.

- **Tastes like:** bergamot, white peach, jasmine
- **Roast:** light, made for filter
- **Price:** €16.50 for 250 g
- **How much:** 30 bags of green coffee, about 1,800 bags roasted

We brew it on a V60 at 15 g to 250 g. It gets sweeter as it cools, so leave it a few minutes before you judge it.

When this lot is gone, it is gone until next year's harvest.
