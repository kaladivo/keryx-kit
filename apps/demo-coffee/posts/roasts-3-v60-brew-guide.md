The question we get most at the roastery is "why does my V60 taste sour?" Usually it is the grind or the pour. Here is the recipe we use for every light roast, including Guji Hambela.

You need 15 g of coffee, 250 g of water just off the boil (about 94 °C), a scale and a timer.

1. **Weigh and grind.** 15 g, medium-fine, about as coarse as sea salt. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and throw that water away.
2. **Bloom.** Pour 40 g of water, enough to wet all the coffee, and wait 40 seconds. You will see it bubble as gas escapes.
3. **Pour to 250 g.** Pour slowly in small circles, keeping the water level steady, and finish by 1:45.
4. **Let it drain.** It should be done by about 3:00.

**If it tastes sour or thin**, grind finer next time. **If it tastes bitter or dry**, or takes longer than 3:30, grind coarser. Change one thing at a time.
