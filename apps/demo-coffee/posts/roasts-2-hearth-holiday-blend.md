Every winter we make one blend for long evenings, and this year it is back on the shelf from **Monday 16 November**.

**Hearth** is two thirds Colombia Los Naranjos and one third a natural Ethiopian from Sidama. Roasted a little darker than our single origins, it works as espresso, in a moka pot or in a French press.

- **Tastes like:** spiced plum, cocoa, orange peel
- **Price:** €15 for 250 g, or €40 for a gift box of three
- **Available:** until 31 December, or until we run out

The red bag is printed by Tiskárna Vlna, three streets from the roastery, on paper that goes in the compost.

Order by 20 December if it should arrive before Christmas.
