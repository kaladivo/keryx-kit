From **Monday 2 November** our checkout uses a new payment provider. You will see a new payment page when you order, still on our website.

**What changes for you**

- Nothing, if you pay at checkout. Enter your card on the payment page as before.
- Subscribers: your saved card moves over automatically. You do not need to enter it again.
- Your bank statement shows **KINDLEWOOD BRNO** instead of the old provider's name.

**What we will never do**

We will never ask you to re-enter your card details, confirm a payment or "verify your account" by email, text message or in this app. Not after this change, not ever.

Fraudsters copy notices like this one. If you get a message that asks for your card, a password or a code and says it is from us, it is not. Delete it.

This channel is different: every message here is signed by Kindlewood, and the Keryx app checks it against our website before showing it to you. If it is in this channel, it is from us.
