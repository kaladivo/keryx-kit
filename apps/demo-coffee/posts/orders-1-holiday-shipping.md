Our team takes a break over the holidays, and so does the roaster.

- **Last roast of the year:** Tuesday 22 December
- **Orders placed by 22 December, 10:00:** roasted that day and shipped on 23 December
- **Orders placed from 22 December, 10:00 to 3 January:** roasted on Monday 4 January and shipped the same day

Couriers are slow in the last week before Christmas. If a bag must arrive by 24 December, order by **Sunday 20 December**.

Coffee subscriptions pause automatically for the week of 28 December. You do not need to do anything, and you will not be charged for that week.

Happy holidays from all five of us.
