We started with a pair we couldn't throw away.

In 2019 Marta wore her favourite sneakers until the sole came off on a tram. Nobody would fix them, because they were glued together to be thrown away. So she and her brother Tomás went looking for a workshop that still stitches soles on.

They found one near Felgueiras, run by the same family for three generations. Today we are eleven people and four shoes, and every pair can come back to us for a new sole. The scuffs you can keep.
