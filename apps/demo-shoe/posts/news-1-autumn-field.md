Every autumn we take one walk outside Felgueiras with a bag of swatches and hold them up against whatever is growing. This year it was a field of wet grass with a rusty tractor parked in it. Hence the name.

**The Rambler** now comes in two new suedes:

- **Moss**, a dark green that hides mud surprisingly well. We tested this.
- **Rust**, a warm brick red that looks better after a few scuffs.

**The Puddle** boot gets a new **Oxblood** waxed suede. Same grippy sole, same pull loop, darker mood.

All three go on sale on **Thursday 29 October** at shoe.roguedave.codes. The Moss Ramblers are a small run of 400 pairs, so if you want them, don't wait for the weekend.

Same price as always: Rambler €135, Puddle €165. Free resoling for the first two years, as with every pair.
