For five years you have bought our shoes without trying them on. That was brave of you. Now you can try them on first.

From **Saturday 14 November to Sunday 20 December** we have a small shop in Karlín, Prague. It used to be a bike workshop. Look for the yellow door.

- **Open:** Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00 to 19:00
- **Opening day:** Saturday 14 November, coffee and pastries from 10:00 while they last
- **In stock:** all four shoes in every size, including the new Moss Ramblers

Tomás will be there most weekends with a brush and a tin of wax. Bring your old pair: we clean them for free, and we take resole orders on the spot.

The pop-up accepts the PUDDLE20 code until it expires on 16 November.

See you at the yellow door.
