06 Security

Keys split by job.

  • Master key

    Lives
    On your owner's Trezor
    Controls
    Who may publish

  • Author keys

    Lives
    Locked by each author's passkey, opened only in their browser
    Controls
    What every message says

  • Channel and publishing keys

    Lives
    On our service, encrypted
    Controls
    Publishing and freshness

We can hold back a message. We can't write one your customers accept.

The honest caveat

Our service sends the console its JavaScript. If we were breached, modified code could capture a key the next time someone signs. We accept this risk for the pilot, and every release publishes the hash of its code.
