Announcements only you can sign.

Keryx replaces email for what your company tells its customers. Every message is signed and checked on their phone.

Request a pilot See how it works

  1. Scan your code.

    Customers scan the QR code on your receipt, newsletter or website, without giving you an email address.

  2. Confirm your domain, once.

    The app shows shoe.roguedave.codes and asks for one check. It is the only one they make.

  3. Every message is signed.

    Your announcement arrives carrying your signature, and the phone checks it before showing a word.

  4. Forgeries never show.

    If your key didn't sign it, it never appears in your feed: no forged sale, no altered post, no warning to click through.

Your marketing team writes. A passkey signs.

Authors write in the browser, see what subscribers will see, and publish with one passkey prompt.

  • Markdown and images Resized in the browser and signed into the post.
  • The subscriber's view The preview uses the app's own sanitizer.
  • No command line Invite authors by link. They sign in with a passkey.

We can hold back a message. We can't write one.

Every key has one job. The keys that write messages are only ever unlocked by the people who hold them.

  • Who may publish

    The master key comes from the owner's Trezor, confirmed on the device. It is never stored.

  • What a message says

    Each author's key is stored locked to their passkey and unlocked only in their browser.

  • What customers see

    Every phone checks each signature against the keys your domain publishes. No match, no message.

What our server holds. Encrypted channel and publishing keys. They let us list, delay or withdraw a message. They can't sign one.

The caveat. Our service sends the console its JavaScript. If our server were compromised, modified code could capture a key the next time someone signs. We accept this for the pilot.
